🌿 Aroma — Give Food a Second Chance

Turning cancelled cloud-kitchen orders into affordable meals.

💡 About Aroma

Aroma is a food-rescue platform designed specifically for cloud kitchens.

Cloud kitchens often prepare food for orders that may later get cancelled or remain uncollected. This can lead to perfectly good food being wasted and financial losses for the kitchen.

Aroma provides a simple solution: cloud kitchens can make their surplus or cancelled orders available to nearby customers at affordable prices.

This creates a win-win situation:

Cloud Kitchens → Recover losses
Customers → Get affordable food
Environment → Less food waste 🌱

🎯 Problem

Cancelled and uncollected orders can result in:

Food wastage
Financial loss for cloud kitchens
Wasted ingredients and resources
Increased environmental impact

There is a need for a platform that can quickly connect this surplus food with customers who are willing to purchase it.

💡 Our Solution

Aroma gives cloud kitchens a dedicated platform to showcase available surplus or cancelled orders.

Customers can discover available food, check its location, view details, and purchase it at a discounted price.
